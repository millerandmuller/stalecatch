Apply
If eligible to apply, you will need to submit the one application pack for both admission to a postgraduate course at the University of Cambridge and a Gates Cambridge Scholarship. The University decides who to admit and Gates Cambridge decides who is offered the Scholarship. Please read this entire section for full information about applying and selection.
Applications open 1 September, for entry in October 2013. Scholarship deadlines are:
Round 1 - US citizens based in the USA - 16 October 2012
Round 2 - all other eligible candidates - 4 December 2012
At a glance
A citizen of any country outside the United Kingdom may apply
Scholarships are available for a postgraduate degree in any subject at the University of Cambridge only - it is not possible to study at any other university
Gates Cambridge does not admit or place scholars - all applications must be made through the University of Cambridge
One application form for postgraduate admission and a Scholarship
